## 5. Services

**H1:** Construction Services in Essex and London
**Intro:** Three service lines: residential building work, commercial fit-out, and surveying. Everything below is delivered directly, not brokered out.

### Residential

**Loft Conversions**
Adding a floor to a house you already own is almost always cheaper than moving. We handle the design, the structural steelwork, building control sign-off and the finish, including dormers, rooflights and staircases. Most conversions add a bedroom and an ensuite; the ones that work best are planned around the staircase position from day one.

**Extensions**
Single storey, double storey, rear, side return and wraparound. The build is the straightforward part — the value is in planning it around how the house is actually used, so you do not end up with a large room nobody sits in. We manage planning applications and party wall matters where they apply.

**Kitchen Refurbishment**
Full strip out through to final fix. Structural openings, steel, electrics, plumbing, plastering, flooring and installation. We can work with a kitchen you have already specified or help you specify one.

**Full Refurbishment**
Whole-property renovation, typically including rewiring, replumbing, new heating, layout changes and full redecoration. Suited to properties being brought back into use or repositioned before sale or let.

**New Build**
Ground-up residential construction, managed end to end from groundworks to handover.

### Commercial

**Office Fit-Out**
Category A and B fit-out, partitioning, services, flooring and finishes. Phased around occupancy where the space stays in use.

**Restaurant Fit-Out**
Front and back of house, including extraction, services and compliance-critical elements. Programmed around opening dates.

### Surveying

**Drone Surveys**
Roof and elevation inspection by drone. No scaffold, no ladders, no access equipment cost. You receive imagery and a written condition summary — useful for insurance claims, pre-purchase checks and planning repairs.

**New Build Snagging Inspections**
Independent inspection of a new-build property before or shortly after completion. We produce a written defect schedule referenced room by room, which you can submit directly to the developer.

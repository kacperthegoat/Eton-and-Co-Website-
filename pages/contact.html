What areas around Norwich do you cover?
We are based in Brundall and cover Norwich and the surrounding villages across Norfolk, including Blofield, Acle, Wroxham, Salhouse, Rackheath, Loddon, Poringland, Thorpe St Andrew and the surrounding rural areas. If you are not sure whether we reach you, give Nick a call and he will confirm straight away.
How does the process work from first contact to finished project?
Get in touch by phone or email and Nick will arrange a free site visit to measure up, check the ground and access, and talk through exactly what you want. You will get a clear, honest quote with no hidden costs. Once you are happy, we schedule the work to suit you and keep you informed throughout.
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes. Every quote from Standfast Fencing is completely free and comes with no obligation at all. Nick will visit the site, assess the job in person, and give you a clear, detailed price so you know exactly what is involved before any work starts.
What types of fencing and timber work do you do?
Close-board, feather-edge, panel and post-and-rail fencing, picket fencing, dog and deer fencing, and bespoke timber gates. We also build decking, sheds, pergolas, arbours, made-to-measure trellis, raised beds and sleeper construction.
Do you clear the garden and take the waste away?
Yes. We can take care of tree and shrub clearance and prepare the garden before the build starts. We are licensed waste carriers, so the old fence, the clearings and all the offcuts leave with us and are disposed of properly. No skip to hire.
Where does your timber come from?
All of our timber is high quality and sustainably sourced. It matters to us, and it is a large part of why the work we do lasts as long as it does.
Are you insured and licensed?
Yes. Standfast Fencing is fully insured and we hold a waste carrier licence covering the clearance and disposal we do as part of our work. You can book with complete confidence.
What are your working hours?
We do not keep set hours. Call or email whenever suits you and Nick will get back to you as soon as he can, usually the same day. He is often out on site, so if he cannot pick up, leave a message or send an email.

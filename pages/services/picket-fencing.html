Picket Fencing

Picket fencing gives you a boundary without closing the garden in. We build ours to measure rather than dropping in off-the-shelf panels, so the pale spacing, height and post caps suit the property. Ideal for front gardens, cottage boundaries and dividing spaces inside a larger garden, and it can be left natural, stained or painted to whatever finish you want.

What Moves the Price

Access. A rear garden with no side access means everything goes through the house, which adds labour.

Structure. Removing load bearing walls means steel, calculations and building control.

Services. Moving a kitchen or bathroom means new runs for waste, water and power.

Specification. The difference between standard and high end finishes is often 20 to 30 per cent.

Ground conditions. Trees, drains and poor soil all affect foundation depth.

Planning. Permitted development is quicker and cheaper than a full application.

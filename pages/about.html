Hi, I’m Liam.

I run Eaton & Co. I’m a third-generation builder. My father and my grandfather were both in the trade, so I have been around sites since I was old enough to carry a bag. Twenty years on, I have worked on just about every side of construction there is.

That means extensions and loft conversions on terraced houses, full refurbishments on properties being brought back into use, ground-up new builds, and commercial fit-out where the building has to stay open while we work. Big jobs and small ones. Both matter, and both get done the same way.

I quote the work, I manage the work, and I am on site while it happens. When something needs deciding, you are talking to the person who can decide it. No layers, no brokered-out trades, no chasing someone who has moved on to the next job.

Our Approach

Timber Work Done Properly

At Standfast Fencing, Nick Holywell-Walker leads every project with a hands-on commitment to quality and craftsmanship. Ask Us Anything
What areas do you cover?
We are based in Brundall and cover Norwich and the surrounding villages across Norfolk. If you are not sure whether we reach you, give Nick a call on 07976 222991 and we will let you know straight away.
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes. Every quote from Standfast Fencing is free and comes with no obligation. Nick will visit the site, measure up, check the ground and access, and give you a clear, detailed price so you know exactly what to expect before any work starts.
What types of fencing do you install?
Close-board and feather-edge for privacy, panel fencing, post-and-rail, picket fencing, and specialist dog and deer fencing. We also make and hang matching timber gates. Everything is supplied and installed, with the old fence taken away.
Is your timber sustainably sourced?
Yes. All the timber we use is high quality and responsibly sourced. It matters to us, and it also means the fence, deck or structure we build for you stands up properly over time.
Do you clear the garden before you start?
We can, and it is usually easier for everyone if we do. Tree and shrub clearance, removing the old fence, and preparing the ground are all part of what we offer. We are licensed waste carriers, so all of it leaves with us and is disposed of properly.
Do you build sheds, pergolas and raised beds too?
Yes. Alongside fencing and gates we build decking, bespoke sheds, pergolas, arbours, made-to-measure trellis, raised beds and sleeper construction. If it is timber and it belongs in a garden, it is worth asking us about.
What are your working hours?
We do not keep set hours. Call or email whenever suits you and Nick will get back to you as soon as he can, usually the same day.
How do I get started?
Call Nick on 07976 222991, email standfastfence@gmail.com, or fill in the contact form on this site. We will get back to you to talk through the project and arrange your free site visit.
